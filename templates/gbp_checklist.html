✅ Your Setup Checklist

Complete these steps in your Google Business Profile. Each one boosts your local search ranking.

1

Claim Your Business

If you haven't already, claim your free listing at business.google.com. Enter your exact business name as it appears on your signage.

2

Add Your Address and Hours

Address: {{ info.get('address', {}).get('street', '') }}, {{ info.get('address', {}).get('city', '') }}, {{ info.get('address', {}).get('region', '') }} {{ info.get('address', {}).get('postal_code', '') }}

Phone: {{ info.get('phone', '') }}

Hours: Mon–Sat, 9:00 AM – 6:00 PM

These must match your website exactly. Google cross-checks consistency.

3

Choose the Right Categories

Primary category: {{ info.get('category', '') }}

Add secondary categories if applicable (e.g., "Appliance Store", "Used Appliance Store"). Categories determine which searches you appear in.

4

Add Photos Weekly

Google prioritizes profiles with fresh photos. Add 2–3 photos per week: new arrivals, the storefront, happy customers (with permission).

Tip: Use photos from your website's product uploads — they're already in the right format.

5

Request Reviews

After every sale, ask the customer for a Google review. Even 20 reviews can move you to the top-3 "Local Pack" results.

Tip: Use the "Reply" button on your reservations page — add "If you enjoyed shopping with us, a Google review would mean a lot" to the end of each reply.

6

Add Products to GBP

Google now lets you list products directly in your Business Profile. Add your top 10 products with photos and prices.

Tip: Copy from your website's product pages — Google may link them automatically.

7

Post Weekly Updates

Google Posts are like mini-announcements on your profile. Post weekly: new arrivals, sales, events. Each post is indexed by Google.

8

Enable Messaging

Turn on Google's messaging feature so customers can text you directly from your profile. Response times matter — set an alert.

📈 Tracking Your Results

Once your GBP is set up, watch your Analytics page for:

  • Directions Clicks — customers clicking "Get Directions" from your storefront
  • Call Clicks — customers tapping to call from their phone
  • Product Views — traffic to your product pages
  • Reservations — high-intent leads

Pro tip: Compare your analytics before and after completing this checklist. Most stores see a 20–50% increase in directions clicks within 30 days.
