📱 What Are QR Codes For?

Print your QR codes and place them where customers will see them:

  • On receipts — customers scan while still in your store
  • On your storefront window — passersby can browse your full inventory
  • On shelf tags — see more photos, sizes, or alternatives
  • On business cards or flyers — one scan brings them to your site
  • On packaging — customers scan when they get home, and come back

Every scan is logged as a "QR Code Scan" in your analytics, so you can see exactly how many customers came from each placement.

Pro tip: Create a separate QR code for each placement (Receipt, Window, Shelf) with its own label. Then compare which one brings the most customers in your Analytics page.

📊 Tracking Your QR Codes

Every scan is recorded in your Analytics page under 📱 QR Code Scans.

To see which placement works best, compare the scans on a weekly basis. If "Receipt" gets 30 scans and "Window" gets 5, you know where to focus your printed materials.
