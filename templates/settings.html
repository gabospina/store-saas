⭐ Google Review Settings

Add your Google Review link here. When a customer completes a purchase, you'll be able to send them a one-click review request from the Reservations page.

How to find your review link:

  1. Open Google Business Profile
  2. Click "Get more reviews"
  3. Copy the short link (looks like https://g.page/r/XXXXX/review)
  4. Paste it above → save

📊 Why Reviews Matter

Google Reviews are one of the top-3 ranking factors for local search. A store with 50 reviews appears higher in "near me" searches than a store with 5 — which means more foot traffic.

When you send a review request from your Reservations page, the customer gets a friendly email with your Google review link. One click, one review, one more reason for Google to promote your store.
