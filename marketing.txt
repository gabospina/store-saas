Your pitch to store owners should always be revenue-focused, not feature-focused:

"You get a professional website + a tool that puts your products on Google Maps and lets 
customers share them on WhatsApp. Your store appears when people search 'clothing store 
near me'. You manage everything from your phone. $X/month, no setup fee."

=======

Strategic thought

Since you're targeting discount stores (not boutiques), you might want to also adjust:

1.  Landing page hero subhead — currently talks about "polished online storefront." Consider: 
"Professional websites for discount clothing stores — starting at $X/month." Speaks directly 
to the audience.

2. Feature descriptions — emphasize cost savings, ease of use, and speed. Not "boutique quality" 
language.

3. Pitch email — when you email a discount store, mention the monthly price early. Discount 
store owners are price-sensitive — they'll want to know "how much?" before anything else.
